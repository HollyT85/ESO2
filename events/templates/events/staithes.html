YFF presents Staithes Rocks @ Staithes Festival of Art & Heritage, 2025

Our first micro-event, designed to bring a family and children’s offer to the village as part of the arts festival. We featured a pop-up festival area on the beach and held activities for two days. Featured Guests included BBC Presenter Professor Chris Jackson, Palaeoartist James McKay and textile workshops from the university of Leeds.
