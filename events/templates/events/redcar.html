YFF presents Redcar Rocks @ Kitefest 2025

The Fossil Festival moves north to Teesside for our new partnership with Redcar and Cleveland Borough Council. Meanwhile an outdoor festival area included geoheritage exhibitors, street artists, musicians, puppeteers and loads more. Over 10000 people visited our event over the course of a single day. Featured Guests included Award Winning Musician Olivia Raffferty, Dance Company - Keisha Dance (Birmingham)
