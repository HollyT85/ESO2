Welcome to Earth Science Outreach UK

Pioneering creative Earth Science education for everyone!

A registered charity, we are dedicated to making Earth Science accessible, relevant and fun.

We exist to help everyone appreciate & understand their local natural world & it’s connection with our planet’s past & future.  We work with communities who may face barriers in accessing science-lead information & activities surrounding earth systems, climate change & sustainability.

We are proud to have a dedicated outreach team of Creative-Scientists who deliver exciting events, workshops, and outdoor activities in schools & community settings.

Our outreach programmes are created & delivered with, and for, cultural & heritage partners. If you would like to sponsor an outreach, develop a partnership or get involved, we’d love to hear from you.
